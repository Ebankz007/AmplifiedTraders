# Amplified Traders — Brand Identity

> Canonical brand book. Source of truth for naming, positioning, voice, colour,
> typography, and platform structure. Distinct design identity from every other
> venture — see the standing rule: one project at a time, one palette per project.

---

## 1. Brand name
**Amplified Traders** — a central community where traders and investors connect,
access trading opportunities, participate in investment packages, copy experienced
traders, and grow through a referral network.

## 2. Positioning
Amplified Traders is a digital trading community — **not a broker** — that connects
people with smarter ways to participate in the financial markets through copy
trading, structured investment packages, referral opportunities, and an active
trader network.

## 3. Core brand promise
**Trade. Copy. Connect. Grow.**
- **Trade** — market participation through the platform
- **Copy** — follow selected strategies and experienced traders
- **Connect** — referral and networking opportunities
- **Grow** — education, community, long-term development

## 4. Slogan
**Primary:** *Your Trading. Your Network. Amplified.*
Alternatives: "Trade. Copy. Connect. Grow." · "Where Traders Connect and Grow." ·
"Trade Together. Grow Together." · "One Platform. Amplified Opportunity." ·
"Connect to Markets. Connect to Opportunity."

## 5. Mission
To create a global community where traders and investors can access trading
opportunities, connect with experienced market participants, and build sustainable
financial knowledge and networks.

## 6. Vision
To become a trusted global community for traders, investors, and financial
communities — where participation is amplified by the network around you.

## 7. Values
**Trust** · **Community** · **Innovation** · **Growth** · **Transparency**

---

## 8. The four business pillars

Each pillar carries the **AMPLIFY** sub-brand.

### AMPLIFY TRADE — *Follow. Learn. Participate.*
Let members follow selected traders and participate alongside them through supported
copy-trading technology.

### AMPLIFY INVEST — *Structured opportunities. Clear expectations.*
Structured participation options designed around different levels and objectives,
with clear terms and full transparency.

### AMPLIFY NETWORK — *Grow your network. Grow your influence.*
Let members build a referral network and participate in a transparent referral
structure that rewards genuine community growth.

### AMPLIFY COMMUNITY — *Trade together. Learn together.*
Bring traders and investors together through education, market discussions,
insights, events, and networking.

## 10. About (short)
Amplified Traders is a digital trading and investment community built to bring
traders, investors, and financial communities together. Our platform combines copy
trading, investment packages, referral networking, and trader education into one
connected ecosystem. Our goal is simple: make participation more accessible, build
stronger trading communities, and help members develop the knowledge and networks
they need to grow.

## 11. Brand personality
Professional · Modern · Global · Trustworthy · Energetic · Community-driven ·
Technology-focused · Growth-oriented.
**Avoid** looking like a traditional forex broker — feel like a global fintech
community/platform.

## 12. Brand colours
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| Primary | Electric Blue | `#2F80ED` | Actions, links, primary brand |
| Reward | Amplified Gold | `#F5B942` | Rewards, milestones, emphasis |
| Base | Deep Navy | `#0B1220` | Page background |
| Surface | Dark Gray | `#171C26` | Cards / elevated surfaces |
| Text | White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text on dark |

Combination: **Navy + Electric Blue + Gold + White** → modern fintech-community
look. Blue is the **primary** (action); gold is the **reward accent** (achievement),
never the base.

## 13. Typography
- **Display:** Space Grotesk — headings, wordmark, numerals
- **Body:** Inter — paragraphs, UI
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono — amounts, IDs, code

## 14. Logo concept
Clean, modern fintech mark. A stylised **"A" + upward movement + connected nodes**.
Must read as **Amplified + Trading + Connection + Growth**. The **network motif**
(connected dots) is the signature visual — it recurs across the site. Keep it simple
— no complex candlestick graphics.
